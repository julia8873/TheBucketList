## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- **My List & Albums**: Full bucket list task creation, custom albums, milestones, progress tracking, and multiple view modes (compact, list, gallery).
- **Feed & Social Moments**: Friends feed, explore ideas feed, interactive likes, saves, and collection grouping.
- **Map View**: Geolocation for goals and experiences with full interactive map visualization.
- **Offline Mode & Sync**: Full offline capability with local SQLite/AsyncStorage persistence and background synchronization.
- **Multi-language Support**: Complete localization in Spanish and English with runtime language switching.
- **Push Notifications**: Expo notifications integration with background delivery and reminder scheduler.
- **Customization**: Dark and Light themes with gold accents, personalized tags with custom emojis, and customizable sorting.

### Security & Privacy
- Zero-permission photo picker integration via Android Photo Picker and iOS PHPicker.
- Explicit blocking of broad media (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`), storage, and overlay permissions to comply with Google Play policies.
- Public legal documentation for Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Account Deletion.
